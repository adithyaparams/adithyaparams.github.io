Kraft bouldering

Start at Kraft Mountain parking (P), not the visitor center. Drive via Calico Basin Road and Sandstone Drive, outside the Scenic Drive.

Mapped trail east into Gateway Canyon
Approximate connection — not a mapped walking path

Use the eastbound trail from the lot. You do not need to complete the full Kraft Mountain Loop or climb over the mountain. Gateway Canyon has rougher terrain and scrambling.

Second Pullout sport climbing

P2 · Calico II is the main parking lot for all four crags. P1 · Calico I is the earlier backup; a 0.4-mile trail adds about 10 minutes to the approach.

The Scenic Drive is one-way. Choose P1 before continuing to P2 if you expect parking to be full. Use designated spaces only.
