1 · Tachycardia

5.73 pitches300 ftfully bolted39.5561, −111.6708

Gear: 12 quickdraws, anchor gear, helmets, and one 60 m rope or longer.

Parking & approach

Use the pullout directly below Heart Rock. Cross the streambed, cut right around the large boulder, and follow the drainage east of Heart Rock. An ammo-can geocache confirms the drainage; bushwhack roughly 50 yards to the toe.

Private-property notice: Mountain Project flags Heart Rock as private property. Check the current notice and obey all closures and parking restrictions before committing.

Pitches

  1. Vena Cava · 5.5 · 30 m: jugs and cobbles to a sloping two-bolt ledge.
  2. The Left Ventricle · 5.6 · 25 m: trend left toward the western headwall belay.
  3. Tachycardia · 5.7 · 30 m: short juggy bulge, exposed headwall, then the protected easy slab to the summit register.

Descent · dedicated line only

Do not rappel the climbing route. From the summit register, find the separate anchors roughly 30 ft north across the cleft. Rappel about 80 ft, then 90 ft—crossing the narrows to anchors on the opposite wall—then 80 ft into the slot and down the ramp.

2 · The Dizzy Channel

5.8−4 pitches270 ftfully bolted39.5560, −111.6875

Gear: 12 quickdraws, anchor gear, helmets, and one 60 m rope if carrying the rappel backup.

Parking & approach

From the Schoolroom-side parking area, walk along the base of Dizzy Rock, pass Fish Pin, and continue beneath the steep overhanging arête. Start on easier terrain just around the arête.

Pitches

  1. The Hidey Hole · 5.7: meandering bolts through clean rock to a pod.
  2. 5.6: climb the arête, cross a narrow ledge, and climb the tower block.
  3. 5.8: step to Dizzy Rock, climb to the short juggy overhang, and belay on Shoe Leather Ledge.
  4. 5.6: follow bolts to chains below the summit; one or two easy fifth-class moves reach the true top.

Preferred descent · summit walk-off

Scramble south over exposed third- and fourth-class terrain to the grassy bench on the west rim of the Left Hand Fork. Follow the faint trail south into the fork, or use the steep northern gully toward the route base only if it is clearly identifiable and safe.

Rappel backup: From the final belay, scramble about 15 ft climber's left to the Fish Pin chains. Descend Fish Pin in three straight rappels with a 60 m rope. Do not improvise if the walk-off is unclear or wet.
